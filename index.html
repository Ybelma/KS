Chapitre I · L’origine

Avant d’être une marque,
Kallos Silya est un territoire.

Dans la vallée de Tafraout, la beauté ne se fabrique pas dans l’urgence. Elle se recueille lentement, au rythme des récoltes, des gestes transmis et des matières que la terre consent à offrir. Nous sélectionnons peu, mais choisissons avec précision.

Chapter II · The territory

Tafraout does not offer abundance.It offers intensity.

Between pink granite, dry winds and almond terraces, every harvest is limited. This scarcity is not a constraint to conceal; it is the source of the concentration, character and value of our ingredients.

Chapter III · Rare materials

Three essences.
One valley.

01

Atlas almond

Terraced orchards

Harvested by hand and pressed at low temperature to preserve the softness and natural richness of its oil.

02

Prickly pear

Minute extraction

Thousands of tiny seeds are required for a small quantity of oil: a naturally rare essence with exceptional sensoriality.

03

Wild argan

Anti-Atlas foothills

Drawn from trees shaped by the dry climate, its oil embodies resistance, nourishment and the memory of the land.

Chapter IV · The gesture

Preserving the hand.
Perfecting the formula.

Kallos Silya joins ancestral knowledge with contemporary cosmetic precision. The gesture remains slow; the formulation becomes exact.

03

Every texture, scent and gesture is composed to turn efficacy into a moment of intimacy.
